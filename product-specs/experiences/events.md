## Step 3: Category Follow-Up — Event Type (reference)

From the original category-tree plan — best starting point for the Step 3 design.

**Title:** Great, what type of event experience are you offering?

| Event Type          | Notes                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Private Celebration | Birthdays, bachelor or bachelorette parties, anniversaries, and social celebrations. |
| Team Outing         | Team activities, client entertainment, offsites, and group bonding experiences.      |
| Entertainment       | Live acts, DJs, games, shows, or interactive entertainment experiences.              |
| Pop-Up Experience   | Temporary, limited-run, branded, seasonal, or location-specific experiences.         |
| Tasting             | Guided tastings of wine, spirits, cocktails, coffee, chocolate, or other food/drink. |
| Workshop            | Hands-on group learning, creative sessions, or instructor-led activities, including cooking and mixology classes. |
| Social Gathering    | Meetups, group experiences, social mixers, or casual private gatherings.             |

> **Internal note — food & drink events:** Tastings and cooking/mixology classes live here as event types — they're group, session-based, host-led happenings, which is exactly what Events holds. Boundary rules with neighboring categories:
>
> - A meal served at a venue → **Restaurant** ([restaurant.md](restaurant.md)).
> - Multi-stop, food-focused routes (market visits, neighborhood food walks) → **Tours → Food Tours** ([tours.md](tours.md)).
> - A private chef cooking at the guest's location → **Service → Chef** ([../service/chef.md](../service/chef.md)).
