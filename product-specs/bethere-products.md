# Bethere Products

## Bethere App

Consumer product for discovering, planning, booking, and managing experiences and services.

## Bethere Admin

Internal Bethere operational admin for managing the overall platform, marketplace, users, partners, content, operations, etc.

Bethere Admin → oversees → entire Bethere ecosystem

## [Hotel] Concierge

Guest-facing white-label experience for a specific hotel.

ℹ️ Bethere App and [Hotel] Concierge are very similar, with some different modules for the hotel context.

## Bethere Provider Portal

Provider-side product for creating and managing listings, availability, bookings, offers, billing/payouts, and business information.

Bethere Provider Portal → supplies inventory/services → Bethere ecosystem

## Bethere Hospitality Portal

Hotel/staff operational product. The admin used to configure and manage the hotel's Concierge experience.

Bethere Hospitality Portal → manages → [Hotel] Concierge

ℹ️ Bethere Provider Portal and Bethere Hospitality Portal are very similar to each other.
