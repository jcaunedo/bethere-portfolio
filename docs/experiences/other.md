**Title:** Tell us more about your experience.

| Experience Type     | Notes                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Custom Experience   | Highly tailored or bespoke experiences built around a guest's request.                |
| Seasonal Activity   | Holiday, festival, summer, winter, or limited-season experiences.                     |
| Adventure           | Active, outdoor, adrenaline, exploration, or challenge-based experiences.             |
| Creative Experience | Art, music, design, photography, craft, or maker-focused experiences.                 |
| Family-Friendly     | Experiences designed for families, children, or mixed-age groups.                     |
| Not Sure Yet        | Use when the provider needs additional guidance before selecting a specific category. |
