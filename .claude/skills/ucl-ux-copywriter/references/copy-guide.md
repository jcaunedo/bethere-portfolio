# BeThere Copy Guide

Use this reference when writing or reviewing BeThere product copy.

## Product Context

BeThere is a curated experiences platform that connects guests with trusted local providers and helps providers create, manage, and publish bookable hospitality experiences. It supports provider onboarding, business verification, experience creation, document collection, pricing, policies, photos, review, publishing, guest discovery, booking requests, and operational follow-through.

BeThere is not a generic vendor directory. It helps providers package their offerings into polished experiences guests can understand, trust, and book.

Useful positioning by context:

- **Emotional:** BeThere helps people find memorable local experiences while giving providers the tools to bring those moments to life.
- **Product:** BeThere is a curated experiences platform for discovering, creating, managing, and publishing bookable hospitality experiences.
- **Enterprise:** BeThere provides provider portal and marketplace infrastructure for onboarding, managing, and publishing hospitality experiences at scale.

## Voice

The default voice is clear, simple, friendly, casual, confident, and helpful.

### Guests

Use a warm, inviting, polished, aspirational, and lightly emotional tone for marketplace, discovery, listing, request, and booking copy. Create desire with concrete details, not hype.

### Providers

Use a guided, practical, friendly, supportive, structured, and low-pressure tone for onboarding, setup, pricing, policies, documents, publishing, and account management.

### Internal Operators

Use a direct, precise, neutral, operational, consistent, and audit-friendly tone for verification, review, approval, compliance, and quality workflows.

Increase warmth and energy for guest experiences, memorable moments, activities, destinations, amenities, and listing quality. Increase restraint for documents, payments, policies, errors, verification, legal details, review, and publishing.

## Core Principles

### Be Clear Before Being Charming

Avoid vague enthusiasm such as `Let's make some magic happen.` Prefer `Create your experience listing.` When the context supports warmth, use `Create an experience guests will love.`

### Describe Outcomes

- `Submit form` -> `Save experience details`
- `Upload compliance files` -> `Add required documents`
- `Proceed to next step` -> `Set your pricing`

### Guide Provider Workflows

- `Enter attributes` -> `Add the amenities guests can expect`
- `Input location` -> `Where will guests meet or board?`
- `Complete all required fields` -> `Add the details guests need before they book`

### Keep Aspirational Copy Grounded

Good copy names the setting, activity, local detail, amenity, or feeling. Avoid unsupported superlatives and generic travel copy. Do not call every experience magical, exclusive, luxury, or unforgettable.

### Reduce Cognitive Load

Use short descriptions, clear CTAs, and guidance placed near the decision. Do not explain every downstream requirement at once.

### Build Confidence

Use accurate status and visibility language such as:

- Verified
- Required before publishing
- Ready to review
- Saved
- Published
- Guests will see this
- Only visible to your team
- You can update this later

### Keep Providers in Control

Favor `Review`, `Edit`, `Save`, `Preview`, `Approve`, `Publish`, and `Update`. Avoid language implying the system or AI made a final decision unless that is literally true.

## Preferred Terminology

### Platform

Prefer:

- Experience
- Provider
- Guest
- Listing
- Booking
- Request
- Publish
- Review
- Preview
- Business details
- Required documents
- Policies
- Pricing
- Availability
- Highlights
- Amenities
- Photos
- Location
- Meeting location
- Boarding location for yacht experiences

Avoid `service` when `experience` fits. Avoid `vendor listing` outside internal contexts. Avoid system terms such as `database`, `record`, `entity`, `object`, or `pipeline` in user-facing copy.

### Guest-Facing

Prefer `Discover`, `Book`, `Request`, `Plan`, `Enjoy`, `Explore`, `Private`, `Curated`, `Trusted`, `Local`, `Guided`, and `Premium` when supported.

Use `Luxury`, `Exclusive`, `Unforgettable`, `Magical`, `Bespoke`, `VIP`, and `Elite` sparingly, if at all.

### Provider-Facing

Prefer `Create`, `Add`, `Set`, `Review`, `Save`, `Publish`, `Confirm`, `Update`, `Upload`, `Complete`, `Required`, and `Optional`.

Avoid `Submit` when a more specific outcome exists. Avoid `Continue` when the next task can be named. Avoid `Configure` for nontechnical users and `Onboard` when speaking directly to providers.

## Component Patterns

### CTAs

Preferred patterns:

- Create experience
- Save changes
- Save and continue
- Add required documents
- Upload photos
- Set pricing
- Add policies
- Preview listing
- Review experience
- Publish experience
- Request booking
- Send request
- Book experience
- Confirm details
- Add business details
- Add boarding location
- Add guest meeting location

Replace generic actions when the next outcome is known:

- `Continue` -> `Set pricing`
- `Submit` -> `Publish experience`
- `Next` -> `Add photos`
- `Done` -> `Save changes`
- `Upload` -> `Upload documents`

### Screen Titles

Orient the user and name the task:

- Create your experience
- Choose an experience type
- Add yacht details
- Add your boarding location
- Set your pricing
- Add cancellation rules
- Add required documents
- Upload photos
- Preview your listing
- Review and publish
- Manage your experiences

Avoid vague titles such as `Details`, `Information`, `Setup`, `Step 1`, `Almost there`, and `Finalize`.

### Descriptions

State what the step is and why it matters. Do not repeat the title.

- Add the details guests need to understand what is included.
- Set pricing by duration, day type, or season.
- Upload photos that show guests what to expect.
- Review your experience before publishing it to BeThere.

### Form Labels

Use short labels in dense forms: `Business name`, `Experience title`, `Boarding location`, `Guest capacity`, `Duration`, `Starting price`, `Cancellation policy`, `Weather policy`, `Minimum age`, `Required documents`.

Use questions in guided flows: `What type of experience are you offering?`, `Where will guests board?`, `What should guests know before they arrive?`, `What is included in this experience?`

### Helper Text

Use helper text to prevent confusion or improve quality:

- Use the name guests will see on BeThere.
- Add the address or marina where guests will board.
- Include anything guests need to know before booking.
- Upload clear photos without watermarks.
- This is required before your experience can be published.

Do not merely restate the label.

## State Patterns

### Empty States

Explain what is missing, why it matters, and what to do next.

```text
No experiences yet
Create your first experience so guests can discover and request it on BeThere.
Create experience
```

```text
No documents uploaded
Add the required documents to verify your business and prepare your experience for publishing.
Upload documents
```

### Errors

Name the issue and recovery path without blame:

- This document could not be uploaded. Try again or choose a different file.
- Expiration date is required before this document can be verified.
- Add a boarding location before publishing this yacht experience.
- Pricing is required before guests can request this experience.

Avoid standalone `Error`, `Invalid input`, `Failed`, `Something went wrong`, and `You entered this incorrectly` when a specific explanation is available.

### Success Messages

Confirm what changed:

- Experience saved.
- Photos uploaded.
- Documents added.
- Pricing saved.
- Business details updated.
- Your experience is published and ready for guest requests.
- Your changes are saved. Guests will see the updated details.

### Warnings

Use warnings for risk, loss, or meaningful consequences:

- Deleting this experience will remove it from BeThere. Existing booking requests will not be deleted.
- This document is required before the experience can be published.
- Guests will not see these changes until you publish the experience.

### Completion States

- **Saved:** Your changes are saved.
- **Ready to review:** Your experience is ready to review.
- **Ready to publish:** All required details are complete. Review your experience before publishing.
- **Published:** Your experience is now live on BeThere.
- **Needs attention:** A few details are needed before this experience can be published.

## AI-Assisted Copy

Keep the provider in control. Prefer `Suggested copy`, `Draft description`, `Recommended details`, `Review before publishing`, and `Edit anything before saving`.

Example:

```text
Review your listing copy
We drafted a description based on your experience details. Edit anything before publishing.
Save listing copy
```

Avoid making AI the headline with phrases such as `AI magic`, `The AI decided`, or `Let AI handle it`.

## Experience Listings

Focus on the moment, setting, activity, amenities, local flavor, inclusions, and what guests can expect. Use short paragraphs and keep operational detail out of the emotional opening.

Preferred structure:

1. Open with the experience value.
2. Add setting, activity, amenity, or flow details.
3. Clarify what guests can enjoy or expect.

Example:

> Enjoy a private yacht experience designed for easy days on the water, skyline views, and time to unwind with your group. Relax on deck, take in the Miami coastline, and arrive with the key charter details already handled.

## Category Language

### Yacht Charters

Use `Yacht experience`, `Yacht charter`, `Boarding location`, `Guest capacity`, `Captain`, `Vessel`, `Amenities`, `Duration`, `Weather policy`, `Safety requirements`, `Commercial insurance`, `Vessel registration`, and `Captain license`.

### Food and Drink

Use `Dining experience`, `Private dining`, `Tasting`, `Reservation`, `Menu`, `Guest count`, `Dietary notes`, `Host`, and `Included items`.

### Tours and Excursions

Use `Tour`, `Excursion`, `Guide`, `Meeting point`, `Duration`, `What's included`, `What to bring`, `Accessibility`, and `Weather considerations`.

### Wellness

Use `Session`, `Treatment`, `Practitioner`, `Location`, `Duration`, `Guest requirements`, and `What to expect`.

### Nightlife

Use `Package`, `Access`, `Reservation`, `Guest count`, `Arrival time`, `Dress code`, `Included items`, and `Host contact`.

## Quality Standard

Make BeThere feel like a modern concierge for guests and a guided business tool for providers: simple enough to move quickly, warm enough for hospitality, precise enough for operations, and polished enough to feel curated rather than generic.
